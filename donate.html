Good to Know

A few honest answers.

Is my gift tax-deductible?

No. These are personal gifts to an individual researcher, not charitable donations to a registered nonprofit, so they do not qualify for a tax deduction. I would rather be clear about that than imply otherwise.

Where exactly does the money go?

Gifts offset the real costs of independent research: interview transcription, participant appreciation, qualitative analysis software, and travel to share findings with the institutions that can use them.

How is my payment handled?

Through PayPal for one-time gifts and Buy Me a Coffee for monthly support. You complete the payment on their secure platforms; this website never collects or stores your financial information.

Can I give in another way?

Yes. If you would like to support the work through your institution or discuss a larger contribution, please reach out directly and we can find the right path.
